FAO (2011) estimates that 12.0% of fruits and vegetables entering supermarket retail in North America and Oceania is lost or wasted there. Producing one kilogram emits about 1.05 kg CO2e (Poore & Nemecek 2018, 'Other Fruit (proxy; avocado not itemised)').
