Cavendish Bananas (bunch) is an ethylene producer — it can speed ripening and yellowing of ethylene-sensitive items stored or boxed with it.  Source: USDA AMS National Organic Program, 'Ethylene — Handling' Technical Report (2023), lines 177-183: bananas, avocado and tomato listed as climacteric; strawberry listed as non-climacteric with 'little or no increase in internal ethylene production'. https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/2023Technical_Report_Ethylene_Handling.pdf
