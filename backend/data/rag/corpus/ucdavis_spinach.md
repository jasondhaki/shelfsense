Spinach is highly sensitive to exogenous ethylene: even low ethylene levels during distribution and short-term storage cause accelerated yellowing. Do not store or display spinach together with ethylene-producing items such as apples, melons and tomatoes.

Wilting, yellowing of leaves and decay increase with storage time, and faster at common distribution temperatures of 5–10 °C; the optimum storage temperature is 0 °C. (Suslow, T. & Cantwell, M., Spinach: Recommendations for Maintaining Postharvest Quality.)
