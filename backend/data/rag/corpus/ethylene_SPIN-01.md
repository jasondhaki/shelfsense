Baby Spinach 5 oz is ethylene-sensitive — keep it away from ethylene producers such as bananas, avocados and tomatoes.  Source: Suslow, T. & Cantwell, M. 'Spinach: Recommendations for Maintaining Postharvest Quality', UC Davis Postharvest Research & Extension Center: spinach is highly sensitive to exogenous ethylene; low levels cause accelerated yellowing; do not mix with apples, melons and tomatoes. https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/node/8161
