FAO (2011) estimates that 2.0% of cereals entering supermarket retail in North America and Oceania is lost or wasted there. Producing one kilogram emits about 1.57 kg CO2e (Poore & Nemecek 2018, 'Wheat & Rye (proxy for bread)').
