Bananas, avocados and tomatoes are climacteric fruits: as they ripen they show a strong peak in respiration with high internal ethylene production, and they respond to exogenous ethylene, which speeds softening and ripening.

Strawberries are non-climacteric: respiration stays roughly constant or declines until senescence, with little or no increase in internal ethylene production. (USDA AMS National Organic Program, Ethylene — Handling Technical Report, 2023.)
